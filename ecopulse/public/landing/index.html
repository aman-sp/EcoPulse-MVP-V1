🌿 About EcoPulse

Delivering Net Zero Realities
for Indian Healthcare

Healthcare contributes nearly 5% of India's total greenhouse gas emissions. Yet most hospitals have no system to measure, track, or report their environmental impact. EcoPulse changes that — with a purpose-built platform that makes sustainability measurable and actionable.

  • ✓
    Automatic Scope 1 & 2 carbon calculations using IPCC-aligned emission factors
  • ✓
    Sustainability scoring from 0–100 with peer benchmarking
  • ✓
    Downloadable compliance-ready reports for NABH, ESG & audits
  • ✓
    Rule-based recommendations tailored to your hospital's performance gaps
  • ✓
    Admin oversight dashboard for multi-hospital networks
🏥

No Measurement = No Improvement

Most Indian hospitals lack any system to track energy, water, waste, or transport emissions — making green goals impossible.

Manual Reporting is Broken
